# Phone Troubleshooting Guide

Simple fixes for common Android and iPhone problems. Start at the top of each section and stop as soon as the problem is solved.

> **Before you start:** back up your photos and important data (Google Photos or Google One on Android, iCloud on iPhone). Never skip this before a reset.

## Quick fixes that solve most problems

1. **Restart the phone.** Hold the power button and choose Restart. This fixes more problems than you would expect.
2. **Update the phone.** Android: *Settings > System > Software update*. iPhone: *Settings > General > Software Update*.
3. **Update your apps** in the Play Store or App Store.
4. **Check free storage.** A phone with less than 10% free space often becomes slow and unstable.

## Phone is slow or freezing

- Restart the phone.
- Close apps you are not using. Android: open the recent apps view and swipe them away. iPhone: swipe up and hold, then swipe apps away.
- Free up space (see "Storage full" below).
- Uninstall apps you no longer use.
- Update the phone and your apps.
- Last resort: back up, then do a factory reset.

## Storage full

- Back up photos and videos to the cloud, then delete them from the phone.
- Android: *Settings > Storage* shows what uses space. Use **Files by Google** to clear junk and large files.
- iPhone: *Settings > General > iPhone Storage* shows each app and offers suggestions.
- Delete downloaded movies, offline music and old chat media (WhatsApp, Telegram).
- Clear an app's cache: Android *Settings > Apps > the app > Storage > Clear cache*. iPhone: offload the app, then reinstall it.

## Wi-Fi not working

1. Check that other devices work on the same Wi-Fi. If they do not, restart the router.
2. Turn Airplane mode on for 10 seconds, then off.
3. Turn Wi-Fi off and on.
4. "Forget" the network and reconnect. You will need the password.
5. Reset network settings. Android: *Settings > System > Reset options > Reset Wi-Fi, mobile and Bluetooth*. iPhone: *Settings > General > Transfer or Reset iPhone > Reset > Reset Network Settings*.

## No signal or mobile data not working

- Toggle Airplane mode on and off.
- Restart the phone.
- Check that mobile data is on and you still have a data plan or balance.
- Remove and reinsert the SIM card, if your phone has a physical SIM.
- Update carrier settings (iPhone) or try another SIM in your phone to test.
- Contact your network provider if there is an outage in your area.

## Battery drains fast

- Check what uses the battery. Android: *Settings > Battery*. iPhone: *Settings > Battery*.
- Lower screen brightness and turn on auto-brightness.
- Turn on Battery Saver (Android) or Low Power Mode (iPhone).
- Close or uninstall apps that use a lot of battery in the background.
- Turn off location access for apps that do not need it.
- Old batteries wear out. On iPhone, check *Settings > Battery > Battery Health*. If it is below about 80%, consider a battery replacement.

## Phone gets too hot

- Take it out of the case and move it out of direct sun or a hot car.
- Stop heavy use (gaming, video recording, charging while gaming).
- Remove the charger and let it cool down. Do not put it in a fridge or freezer.
- Update apps, since a stuck app can overwork the processor.
- If it overheats while idle, or the battery looks swollen, stop using it and see a repair shop.

## App keeps crashing

1. Close the app fully and reopen it.
2. Update the app, then update the phone.
3. Clear the app's cache (Android) or offload and reinstall it (iPhone).
4. Uninstall and reinstall the app. Check first that your data is backed up in the app account.
5. Contact the app's support if it still crashes.

## Screen not responding

- Remove the screen protector or case if it is thick or damaged, and clean the screen.
- Make sure your hands are dry and clean.
- Force restart. Android: hold power for about 10 to 30 seconds (some models: power + volume down). iPhone with Face ID: press volume up, volume down, then hold the side button.
- If a cracked screen is the cause, see a repair shop.

## Can't charge

- Try a different cable and a different charger. Cables fail often.
- Clean the charging port gently with a wooden or plastic toothpick. Never use metal.
- Restart the phone and try again.
- If it still will not charge, the port or battery may need repair.

## Forgot the passcode or locked out

- Android: use "Forgot pattern or PIN" and sign in with your Google account, or use Google Find Hub to erase and reset.
- iPhone: after too many tries the phone locks. Use the Erase iPhone option on the lock screen or recovery mode, then restore from your iCloud backup.
- Both methods can erase the phone, so a backup matters.

## Factory reset (last resort)

Only do this after you have backed up your data and tried the fixes above.

- Android: *Settings > System > Reset options > Erase all data (factory reset)*.
- iPhone: *Settings > General > Transfer or Reset iPhone > Erase All Content and Settings*.

## When to get professional help

- The screen is cracked or has lines or dead spots.
- The battery is swollen, very hot or leaking.
- The phone has water damage and will not turn on. Do not charge it. Turn it off and see a repair shop.
- Problems come back after a factory reset.

## Stay safe

- Install apps only from the Play Store or App Store.
- Do not tap links in unexpected messages.
- Keep automatic updates on.
- Use a screen lock (PIN, fingerprint or face).
